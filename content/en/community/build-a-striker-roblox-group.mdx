## What Is Build A Striker?

Build A Striker is a fan-made, unofficial Roblox game created by "huge Blue Lock fans" as a tribute to the anime. It's a sports game (Sports & Racing genre) supporting up to **12 players per server**, and it has already passed **1.1 million visits** with a strongly positive vote ratio — nearly 8,000 upvotes against just 390 downvotes.

The core loop, straight from the official description:

1. **Roll a random team**, ranging from First Selection all the way up to Pros.
2. **Receive a random player** from that team.
3. **Choose ONE of the player's 10 attributes**: Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, or Weapon.
4. Play through **10 rounds**, building your ultimate striker one part at a time.
5. **Climb the leaderboard** and prove you're the #1 Egoist.

It's an RNG-driven squad builder — every roll is a gamble, and picking which single attribute to keep is where the strategy lives.
