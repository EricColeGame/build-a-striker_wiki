If you're looking for a Build A Striker progression guide, here's the honest starting point: the game doesn't have XP, levels, currencies, or unlock trees. Progression in this Roblox soccer game is built entirely around its core loop — rolling a random team, picking one attribute from a random player, and doing that over 10 rounds to climb the leaderboard. Everything below sticks to what the official game page confirms, so you know exactly what to expect before you queue up.

## What Build A Striker Is

[Build A Striker](https://www.roblox.com/games/98256320592611/) (full title: [🧪 CHEMISTRY] Build A Striker ⚽) is a fan-made Roblox game by the group Wild Card., created as a tribute to the anime Blue Lock. It launched in early October 2026 and has already passed a million visits, with a healthy like ratio and several thousand favorites as of late October 2026.

The premise matches its title: you build the ultimate striker, one part at a time. It supports up to 12 players per server and falls under the Sports genre on Roblox.

## The Core Progression Loop, Step by Step

The official game page lays out the loop clearly. Each session, you'll follow this sequence:

1. **Roll a random team.** The pool ranges from First Selection all the way up to Pros — tiers Blue Lock fans will recognize.
2. **Get a random player** from that team. RNG decides who you pull.
3. **Choose ONE of their 10 attributes.** Every player has the same stat set: Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, or Weapon.
4. **Repeat across 10 rounds**, then compare your finished striker against everyone else on the leaderboard.

That's the entire progression frame. Ten rounds, one attribute choice per round, and a leaderboard at the end to prove you're the "#1 Egoist."

## The 10 Attributes and What They Mean

Every random player you roll offers the same ten attribute slots:

- Shooting
- Speed
- Dribbling
- Passing
- Physical
- Defense
- Vision
- IQ
- Ego
- Weapon

One thing worth flagging: the developer has not published numeric values for these attributes, nor any official tier list ranking players or stats. If you see claims about exact stat math, treat them as speculation. The strategic layer comes from *which* attribute you take from *which* rolled player — and since teams are rolled randomly, adapting your picks round to round matters more than memorizing a fixed build.

## What "Progression" Actually Looks Like

Because there's no documented XP, level, reward, unlock, or battle-pass system, your progression between sessions is measured in one thing: leaderboard standing. Each 10-round run is a fresh chance to assemble a stronger striker and post a better result.

If you're used to games where grinding buys you permanent power, this is a reset-each-run model. The fun is in the RNG drafting — sometimes you roll a Pros-tier team and get a standout player; sometimes you're working with an early First Selection pull and have to make the smartest of ten imperfect choices.

## Quick Tips for Your First Run

- **Expect randomness, plan for it.** Your team and player rolls are out of your hands; your attribute choice is the only lever you control.
- **Don't tunnel-vision on Shooting.** Ten different attributes exist for a reason, and variety across rounds may serve your build better than stacking one stat.
- **Use all 10 rounds.** Every round is another attribute pick — skipping chances to refine your striker only hurts your leaderboard shot.
- **Check the community server** the developers mention for active codes and patch news.

## FAQ

**Is there a level or XP system in Build A Striker?**
No. The official game page documents no XP, levels, rewards, unlocks, or battle pass. Progression is the 10-round loop and the leaderboard.

**How many rounds are in a Build A Striker match?**
Ten rounds. Each round you roll a team, get a random player from it, and pick one of that player's 10 attributes for your striker.

**What attributes can I choose from in Build A Striker?**
Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, and Weapon — the same ten for every player you roll.

**Is Build A Striker an official Blue Lock game?**
No. It's a fan-made, unofficial tribute created by Blue Lock fans. The Blue Lock franchise itself is separate — you can find official series information on [Crunchyroll](https://www.crunchyroll.com/), which streams the anime.
