## What Is Build A Striker?

Build A Striker is a fan-made Roblox soccer game inspired by the Blue Lock anime, created by the group **Wild Card.** (owned by RemedyGod). The official title currently listed on Roblox is **[🧪 CHEMISTRY] Build A Striker ⚽**, and it falls under the Sports & Racing genre with support for up to 12 players per server.

Despite only launching on October 2, 2026, the game has already surpassed 1.1 million visits with nearly 8,000 favorites and a strongly positive vote ratio, according to its official Roblox page. The core promise is simple: build the ultimate striker, one part at a time, then prove you're the #1 Egoist on the leaderboard.

## How the Core Loop Works

The gameplay follows a roll-and-choose structure that repeats across a match. Here's the verified loop straight from the official game description:

1. **Roll a random team** — pulls range from the First Selection tier all the way up to Pros.
2. **Get a random player** from that rolled team.
3. **Choose ONE of the player's 10 attributes** to add to your striker.
4. **Repeat across 10 rounds**, then climb the leaderboard based on how your build performs.

Because every roll is randomized (RNG), no two runs play out the same way. Your striker is assembled piece by piece, and the tension comes from deciding which single attribute to take from each player you roll.

## The 10 Attributes Explained

Each player you roll offers one of ten attributes to choose from:

- Shooting
- Speed
- Dribbling
- Passing
- Physical
- Defense
- Vision
- IQ
- Ego
- Weapon

The developers have not publicly documented the numeric effect of each attribute, so we can't tell you which stat is objectively strongest. What's clear is that the decision matters: you can only take one attribute per player, so a "player quality vs. attribute choice" trade-off sits at the heart of every round.

## Chemistry, Rounds, and the Leaderboard

The current version of the game highlights a **Chemistry** mechanic in its title, though the official description doesn't detail exactly how chemistry is calculated. The match structure is straightforward: **10 rounds** per run, after which your final striker is judged and you climb the LEADERBOARD.

The spirit of competition is pure Blue Lock — the game even challenges you to prove you're the "#1 Egoist." With 12-player servers, you're directly competing against other rolling strikers in the same lobby.
