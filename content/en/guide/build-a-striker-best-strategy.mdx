## What Build A Striker Is (and How It Works)

Build A Striker is a fan-made Roblox soccer game by the group Wild Card., created as a tribute to the anime Blue Lock. The core loop is simple: you roll a random team — ranging from First Selection all the way up to Pros — get a random player from that team, and then choose **one** of that player's 10 attributes to add to your striker. After 10 rounds, you climb the leaderboard and try to prove you're the #1 Egoist.

Because every roll is random, the best strategy isn't about memorizing a fixed build. It's about making smart, consistent decisions with whatever the RNG hands you. This guide breaks down that decision-making using only what the game officially documents.

## The 10 Attributes You Can Pick From

Every player you roll comes with the same 10 attribute categories:

- Shooting
- Speed
- Dribbling
- Passing
- Physical
- Defense
- Vision
- IQ
- Ego
- Weapon

Since you can only take **one attribute per player**, the game is fundamentally a draft: each round forces a trade-off. The strategic question is always the same — which single attribute from this random player improves my striker the most?

## Build A Striker Best Strategy: Core Principles

While the game doesn't publish numeric attribute effects, the loop itself reveals the strategic pillars:

**1. Build around a striker identity.** A striker in a soccer/Blue Lock-style game lives and dies by finishing. Prioritizing Shooting when it's available on a strong roll keeps your build coherent. Speed and Dribbling complement it, while Defense and Passing serve different identities.

**2. Don't panic-fill every round.** With only 10 rounds, taking a mediocre attribute just to "use" the round can be worse than holding your theme. Consistency across rounds is what the leaderboard rewards.

**3. Read the team tier before you pick.** Teams roll from First Selection up to Pros. A player from a higher-tier team is more likely to be worth any attribute from — so a mid-tier attribute from a Pros-level player can beat a great attribute from a weak roll.

**4. Treat Ego and Weapon as swing picks.** These Blue Lock-flavored attributes are the wildcard slots in the kit. Without documented numbers, the safest approach is to experiment across rounds and see how they change your striker's feel before committing to them in serious leaderboard runs.

## Managing the RNG

The game is explicitly roll-based (the official description tags it with "roll" and "rng"), so luck plays a real role. The strategy layer is minimizing regret:

- Decide your attribute priority order **before** you start rolling, so each pick is fast and disciplined.
- Expect variance — 10 rounds is a short draft, and one bad streak of teams can sink a run. Play multiple runs rather than tilting on one.
- The game was updated recently (its official record shows updates as of October 29, 2026), so re-check the game page for any mechanic changes that could shift priorities.
