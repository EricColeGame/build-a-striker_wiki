## What Is Build A Striker?

Build A Striker is a fan-made Roblox soccer game by the group **Wild Card.**, built as a tribute to the anime *Blue Lock*. The core idea is simple: you construct the ultimate striker one part at a time, over a run of 10 competitive rounds. Matches support up to 12 players, and since launching on October 2, 2026, the game has already passed 1.1 million visits with roughly 1,800 players online at the time of writing — so it's a fresh, active experience worth learning quickly.

This beginner guide sticks to what's officially documented, so you know exactly what you're getting into before your first roll.

## The Core Gameplay Loop, Step by Step

Every round follows the same structure, straight from the game's official description:

1. **Roll a random team** — draws range from First Selection (the lowest tier) all the way up to Pros.
2. **Get a random player** from that rolled team.
3. **Choose ONE of the player's 10 attributes** to add to your striker.
4. **Repeat across 10 rounds**, then climb the leaderboard.

The tension comes from RNG: you don't control which team or player appears, only which single attribute you keep. A "bad" roll can still deliver one elite trait, so every round matters.

## The 10 Attributes You Can Pick

Each player you roll offers one of ten attribute choices. Here's the full official list:

- Shooting
- Speed
- Dribbling
- Passing
- Physical
- Defense
- Vision
- IQ
- Ego
- Weapon

The game doesn't publish numeric attribute effects or a tier list, so avoid guides claiming exact stat values — those aren't documented. Think of the choices as building a well-rounded striker over 10 picks rather than perfecting any single number.

## Team Tiers: First Selection Through Pros

Rolls are weighted across team tiers, starting at **First Selection** and rising to **Pros**. Higher-tier teams presumably hold stronger players, but the official page doesn't document drop rates or how tiers influence attribute quality — treat any specific numbers you see elsewhere as unconfirmed. What's confirmed is the progression framing: the further up the tier ladder your roll lands, the more hyped the pick.

The *Blue Lock* theme runs deep here — "First Selection" and "Ego" are direct nods to the anime, and the leaderboard challenge asks you to prove you're the "#1 Egoist."

## Quick Beginner Tips (What's Actually Verified)

- **Play all 10 rounds** — that's the full format, and leaderboard ranking comes from complete runs.
- **Read your roll before picking** — with only one attribute per round, a careless choice can't be undone.
- **Expect variance** — RNG is the whole point of the roll system, from team tier to player.
- **Like and favorite the game** — the developer explicitly asks for this to support it.
