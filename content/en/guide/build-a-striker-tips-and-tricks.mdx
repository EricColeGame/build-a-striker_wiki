## What Is Build A Striker?

[Build A Striker](https://www.roblox.com/games/98256320592611/) is a fan-made Roblox soccer game inspired by Blue Lock, created by the group Wild Card. The core idea is simple: you build the ultimate striker one part at a time by rolling random players and taking a single attribute from each one. Across 10 rounds, your choices stack up into a finished striker, and the best builds climb the leaderboard to prove who is the #1 Egoist.

Because the game leans on RNG (random rolls), the biggest skill gap comes from how you use the one pick you're allowed each round. That's where these Build A Striker tips and tricks come in.

## How the Game Loop Works

Every round follows the same verified flow, straight from the official game description:

1. **Roll a random team** — teams range from First Selection all the way up to Pros, so the tier of your roll varies.
2. **Get a random player from that team** — once the team is set, one of its players is drawn for you.
3. **Choose ONE of that player's 10 attributes** — Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, or Weapon.
4. **Repeat across 10 rounds**, then compare your completed striker on the leaderboard.

Matches support up to 12 players, and the game is updated regularly — it launched in early October 2026 and has already passed one million visits.

## Attribute Tips: What Each Pick Means

The 10 attributes are officially confirmed, but the developers have not published numeric effects, so treat any tier list you see elsewhere as unconfirmed. What we do know is how to think about the categories:

- **Attacking stats** — Shooting, Dribbling, and Speed map directly to a striker's core job. If your goal is a pure goalscorer, these are your bread and butter.
- **Support stats** — Passing, Vision, and IQ shape how a striker creates rather than just finishes.
- **Physical and Defense** — Physical covers a striker's body and presence; Defense is an unusual pick for a striker build, so think carefully before spending a round on it.
- **Ego and Weapon** — These are the Blue Lock-flavored picks, and they're clearly meant to be the identity of your build.

## Build A Striker Strategy Tips

Since you only get one attribute per round, decision-making is everything:

- **Plan your build path before you roll.** Decide early whether you're going for a pace-and-finish striker or a playmaking egoist, and prioritize attributes that fit that identity across your 10 picks.
- **Don't waste picks on redundancy.** Ten rounds means ten chances to shape your striker — spreading picks across too many attributes can leave your build unfocused.
- **Remember the roll is random.** A high-tier team (Pros) gives you a player from that tier, but you still get a random player from it. Bad rolls happen; your job is to extract the best possible attribute from whatever shows up.
- **Play all 10 rounds.** The leaderboard is decided over the full run, so a weak start isn't a reason to give up on the round set.
- **Like and favorite the game** if you enjoy it — the developer asks for this support directly.
