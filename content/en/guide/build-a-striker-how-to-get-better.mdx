## What Build A Striker Is

Build A Striker is a fan-made Roblox soccer game created by the group Wild Card., inspired by the Blue Lock anime. It has quickly picked up traction since launching in early October 2026, crossing one million visits with thousands of players jumping in each session. If you're wondering how to get better, the honest answer starts with understanding the game's core loop — because improvement here is less about reflexes and more about making smart decisions, one pick at a time.

The official game description lays out the loop clearly: you build the ultimate striker one part at a time across ten rounds, then climb the leaderboard to prove you're the #1 Egoist.

## How the Core Loop Works

Every round follows the same verified flow, so knowing it inside and out is the foundation of getting better:

- **Roll a random team** — the pool ranges from First Selection all the way up to Pros.
- **Get a random player** from that rolled team.
- **Choose ONE of that player's 10 attributes** to add to your striker: Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, or Weapon.
- **Play 10 rounds total**, then see how your build stacks up on the leaderboard.

Each round gives you exactly one attribute choice from one random player. That constraint is the whole game — and it's where better players separate themselves.

## How to Get Better: Make Deliberate Choices

Because you can only take one attribute per round, every pick matters. The developers haven't published numeric attribute effects or a tier list, so treat any claim about "best stats" you see elsewhere as unconfirmed. What you can control is your approach:

- **Decide on an identity early.** Ten rounds means roughly ten attribute slots. A striker who alternates randomly between Defense, IQ, and Shooting will likely end up less cohesive than one committed to a scoring-focused or dribbling-focused build.
- **Pay attention to what each roll offers.** You get a random player from a random team, so part of getting better is evaluating what's actually on the table each round instead of picking on autopilot.
- **Don't ignore the non-obvious stats.** Attributes like Vision, IQ, Ego, and Weapon exist alongside the classic soccer stats. The official description doesn't explain their exact effects, so experimenting across multiple 10-round runs is the reliable way to learn what they do for your build.

## Manage the RNG, Don't Fight It

Build A Striker is built around rolling — the game's own tags include "roll" and "rng." You can't choose which team or which player appears, which means variance is baked in. Better players accept this and optimize the one thing they fully control: the attribute selection.

A practical mindset: treat bad rolls as information. If you keep getting offered players weak in your target attribute, you'll learn to adapt your build direction mid-run rather than tunnel-visioning on a plan the RNG won't allow. Over multiple runs, you'll develop a feel for which attribute is worth taking when your first choice isn't available.
