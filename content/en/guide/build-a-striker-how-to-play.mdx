## What Is Build A Striker?

**Build A Striker how to play** comes down to one core idea: build the ultimate striker one part at a time. The game — currently titled [🧪 CHEMISTRY] Build A Striker ⚽ — is a fan-made, unofficial Roblox soccer game created by the group **Wild Card.** (owned by RemedyGod) as a tribute to the anime *Blue Lock*.

The concept is a round-based RNG squad builder. You roll for random teams, pull a random player, and claim just one attribute from that player. After 10 rounds, the strongest build wins. Since launching on October 2, 2026, it has drawn over 1.1 million visits and consistently holds more than 1,800 concurrent players.

You can jump in directly via the [official Build A Striker game page on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/98256320592611/).

## How to Play Build A Striker: Step by Step

The official game description lays out a simple five-step loop:

1. **Roll a random team** — results range from First Selection all the way up to Pros, so your rolls vary in quality.
2. **Get a random player** from that team. You don't choose the player; the RNG does.
3. **Choose ONE of their 10 attributes.** This is the strategic heart of the game — you can only take a single stat from each player you pull.
4. **Repeat across 10 rounds**, stacking one attribute per round onto your striker.
5. **Climb the leaderboard** and prove you're the #1 Egoist.

Because you only get one attribute per roll, every decision matters. Passing up a great stat this round means it's gone — your next roll is a completely new team and a completely new player.

## The 10 Attributes You Can Pick

Each rolled player exposes exactly ten attributes, and you may take only one:

- Shooting
- Speed
- Dribbling
- Passing
- Physical
- Defense
- Vision
- IQ
- Ego
- Weapon

The official page does not document how these attributes are weighted numerically or which produce the strongest builds, so treat any "best attribute" claims you see elsewhere as unconfirmed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do you play Build A Striker on Roblox?

You play by rolling a random team (from First Selection up to Pros), receiving a random player from that team, and choosing one of that player's 10 attributes — such as Shooting, Speed, or Ego. This repeats across 10 rounds as you build your ultimate striker and climb the leaderboard.

### How many attributes can you pick per player in Build A Striker?

Just one. Each rolled player offers 10 attributes to choose from — Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, and Weapon — but you can only claim a single attribute per round.

### Is Build A Striker an official Blue Lock game?

No. The developers state clearly that it's a fan-made, unofficial game created by Blue Lock fans as a tribute to the anime. It has no official affiliation with the Blue Lock franchise.

### How many rounds are in a game of Build A Striker?

A full game lasts 10 rounds. After the final round, players compare builds and compete for leaderboard positions as the top "Egoist."
