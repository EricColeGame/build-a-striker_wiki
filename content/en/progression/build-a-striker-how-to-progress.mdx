## How Progression Works in Build A Striker

If you're wondering how to progress in Build A Striker, the answer is refreshingly simple: you play through a 10-round match and climb the leaderboard. That's the verified core loop straight from the game's official Roblox page. There is no documented XP system, level grind, battle pass, or unlock tree — your progression is measured by how strong your striker gets across the rounds and where you finish on the leaderboard.

Here's the flow you can expect each match:

1. **Roll a random team** — pulls range from First Selection all the way up to Pros.
2. **Receive a random player** from that team.
3. **Choose ONE of that player's 10 attributes** to add to your striker.
4. **Repeat across 10 rounds**, building your ultimate striker one part at a time.
5. **Climb the leaderboard** and prove you're the #1 Egoist.

## The 10 Attributes You Can Pick From

Every rolled player gives you a choice of one attribute, and knowing the full list helps you plan your build instead of picking blindly:

- Shooting
- Speed
- Dribbling
- Passing
- Physical
- Defense
- Vision
- IQ
- Ego
- Weapon

The game doesn't publicly document the numeric effect of each attribute or offer an official tier list, so treat any tier list you see elsewhere as community opinion, not fact. The strategic layer comes from adapting to what the RNG gives you — since both the team and the player are random, flexibility matters more than locking into one "best" stat.

## Progression Is Per-Match, Not Account-Wide (As Far as Documented)

One important clarification: the official game page documents the 10-round match structure and the leaderboard, but it does not document any persistent XP, levels, rewards, or unlock systems. That means, based on verified information, your "progress" within a session is the striker you assemble over those 10 rounds, and your longer-term progress is your standing on the leaderboard.

If you see videos or posts claiming specific level rewards or milestone bonuses, be cautious — those claims aren't supported by the official documentation as of October 29, 2026. The official description does point players toward the community server for codes and updates, so that's the best place to catch new features as they're announced.

## Tips for Making the Most of Your 10 Rounds

While hard numbers aren't documented, the structure itself suggests a few sound habits:

- **Read the roll before committing.** Since teams range from First Selection to Pros, higher-tier rolls give you a chance at stronger attributes — but the player you get is random within that team.
- **Think about your build shape.** With 10 picks across 10 attributes, you can either specialize hard or spread across categories. Decide early whether you're chasing a specific identity for your striker.
- **Stay flexible with RNG.** The loop is random by design, so the players who progress furthest are usually the ones who adapt rather than force a predetermined plan.
- **Remember Ego and Weapon exist.** These Blue Lock-flavored attributes are part of the official pool, and they're what make the "#1 Egoist" framing more than just flavor.

## FAQ

**How do you progress in Build A Striker?**
You progress by playing the 10-round loop: roll a team, get a random player, add one of their 10 attributes to your striker, and repeat. Your results determine your leaderboard position.

**Is there an XP or level system in Build A Striker?**
Not that's documented. The official game page confirms the 10-round structure and leaderboard but lists no XP, levels, rewards, or unlocks. Don't trust claims of level milestones without official confirmation.

**How many rounds are in a Build A Striker match?**
Ten rounds. Each round gives you one new attribute choice for your striker.

**What attributes can I choose in Build A Striker?**
Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, and Weapon — ten total, one pick per round.
