## How Progression Actually Works in Build A Striker

Build A Striker is a fan-made Blue Lock tribute on Roblox where you construct the ultimate striker one piece at a time. The verified core loop, straight from the official game description, is simple:

1. **Roll a random team** — draws range from First Selection all the way up to Pros.
2. **Receive a random player** from that rolled team.
3. **Choose ONE of that player's 10 attributes**: Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, or Weapon.
4. **Repeat across 10 rounds**, building your striker piece by piece.
5. **Climb the leaderboard** and prove you're the #1 Egoist.

That's the entire documented progression system. There's no persistent meta-level to grind between sessions — your power within a run is determined by the choices you make round by round.

## The Fastest Way to "Level Up": Optimize Your 10 Picks

Since each round gives you exactly one attribute from one randomly rolled player, your speed of improvement comes from decision quality, not grinding. A few principles that follow directly from the verified loop:

- **Understand the roll tiers.** Teams roll from First Selection through Pros. Higher-tier rolls give you access to stronger players, which means stronger attribute options.
- **Treat every pick as a build decision.** With 10 attributes to choose from, each selection shapes your striker's identity. Pick deliberately rather than automatically.
- **Think in rounds, not sessions.** A full run is 10 rounds. The faster you make confident, coherent choices, the faster you complete a run and push your leaderboard score.
