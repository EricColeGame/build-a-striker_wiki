## The Short Answer on Build A Striker Unlocks

If you're searching for Build A Striker unlocks, here's the honest answer: as of October 29, 2026, the game's official Roblox page does not document an XP system, levels, currencies, reward tracks, or a battle pass of any kind. The verified progression loop is simpler — you play through 10 rounds per run and climb the leaderboard to prove you're the "#1 Egoist."

That doesn't mean there's nothing to chase. It means your progression in [Build A Striker](https://www.roblox.com/games/98256320592611/) is measured in leaderboard standing and the quality of the striker you assemble each run, not in persistent unlocks.

## How Progression Actually Works

Build A Striker is a fan-made Blue Lock tribute created by the Roblox group Wild Card., and its core loop is built around rounds rather than a grindable progression system:

1. **Roll a random team** — draws range from First Selection all the way up to the Pros tier.
2. **Get a random player** from that rolled team.
3. **Choose ONE of that player's 10 attributes** to add to your striker.
4. **Repeat across 10 rounds**, then compare your finished striker on the leaderboard.

The 10 rounds function as the game's progression frame. Each pick compounds — every attribute you take shapes whether your final striker is a sniper, a speedster, or something more balanced.
