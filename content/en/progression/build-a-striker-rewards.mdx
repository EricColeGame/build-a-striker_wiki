## What "Rewards" Actually Mean in Build A Striker

If you're searching for Build A Striker rewards, here's the honest answer up front: the game's official Roblox page does not document any XP system, level curve, unlock track, currency, or battle pass. There is no confirmed reward shop or milestone chest to grind toward.

What the game does offer is a clear, competitive progression frame: a **10-round match structure** and a **leaderboard**. Your reward for playing well is rank — climbing the board and proving you're the "#1 Egoist," in the developer's own framing. Any claim about specific in-game rewards beyond that isn't backed by the official description as of October 29, 2026.

## How Progression Works Round by Round

Build A Striker is a fan-made Blue Lock tribute built by the group Wild Card., and its loop is simple but competitive. Each session plays out like this:

1. **Roll a random team** — your options range from First Selection all the way up to the Pros tier.
2. **Receive a random player** from that rolled team.
3. **Choose ONE of that player's 10 attributes**: Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, or Weapon.
4. **Repeat across 10 rounds**, building the ultimate striker one part at a time.
5. **Compare your finished striker** against other players and climb the leaderboard.

In other words, the "reward" structure is baked into the drafting itself. Every roll is a mini-payout, and every attribute pick is a decision that shapes your final build.

## Leaderboard Climbing: The Verified Reward System

The leaderboard is the only confirmed reward ladder in Build A Striker. Ten rounds per session, a finished striker at the end, and a ranking to defend. Because matches are capped at 12 players per server, your competition pool is small and immediate — improving your attribute picks directly affects where you land.

The game is genuinely popular, which keeps that leaderboard meaningful: as of October 29, 2026, it has surpassed 1.1 million visits with roughly 1,850+ concurrent players and an overwhelmingly positive like ratio (about 7,900 up votes versus 390 down votes).
