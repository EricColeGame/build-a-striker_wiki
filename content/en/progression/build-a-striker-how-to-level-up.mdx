## Does Build A Striker Have a Leveling System?

If you're searching for how to level up in Build A Striker, here's the honest answer up front: the game does not document an XP bar, player levels, unlock trees, or a battle pass. What it does have is a clear, round-based progression structure — 10 rounds per run where you build your striker attribute by attribute, then climb the leaderboard to prove you're the #1 Egoist. In practice, "leveling up" in this game means making better picks across those 10 rounds, not grinding experience points.

The game is a fan-made Roblox tribute to Blue Lock, created by the group Wild Card., and it's been gaining serious traction — over 1.1 million visits since its October 2026 launch, with roughly 1,800 players in-game at the time of writing.

## How the Core Loop Works

Every run follows the same verified structure, per the [official Roblox game page](https://www.roblox.com/games/98256320592611/):

1. **Roll a random team** — the pool ranges from First Selection all the way up to Pros tiers.
2. **Get a random player** from that rolled team.
3. **Choose ONE of that player's 10 attributes**: Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, or Weapon.
4. **Repeat for 10 rounds**, building your striker one part at a time.
5. **Compare against the leaderboard** at the end of the run.

Because each round only lets you take a single attribute, every pick is a trade-off. That's the real skill expression in the game — there's no separate progression menu to optimize.

## How to "Level Up" Faster: Practical Tips

While the mechanics below stick to documented features, a few habits will naturally improve your results across the 10-round format:

- **Build around a identity, not a checklist.** You can't max all 10 attributes, so decide early whether you're chasing a shooting-focused finisher or a speed-and-dribbling dribbler, and commit your picks to that plan.
- **Treat Ego and Weapon as tiebreakers.** These Blue Lock-flavored attributes are part of the official pool. Since the game doesn't publish numeric attribute effects, experiment to see how they shift your build rather than assuming a meta.
- **Play full 10-round runs.** The leaderboard is the progression frame — partial runs mean fewer attributes banked and a weaker final striker.
- **Watch the team tiers you roll.** The roll pool spans First Selection through Pros. Higher-tier rolls give you access to different players and attribute quality, so adapt your plan to what the RNG hands you.

## FAQ

### How do you level up in Build A Striker?

There is no XP or level system documented in the game. Progression works through 10 rounds per run, where you pick one attribute per round from 10 options (Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, or Weapon), then climb the leaderboard with your final striker.

### What are the 10 attributes in Build A Striker?

The officially listed attributes are Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, and Weapon. You choose exactly one from each player you roll during a round.

### Does Build A Striker have codes?

The official game description tells players to join the community server for codes and updates, but no codes are published on the official Roblox pages themselves. Check the developer's official channels rather than trusting third-party code lists.

### Who made Build A Striker?

It was created by Wild Card., a Roblox group owned by RemedyGod, as an unofficial fan tribute to the Blue Lock anime. It launched in October 2026 and supports up to 12 players per server.
