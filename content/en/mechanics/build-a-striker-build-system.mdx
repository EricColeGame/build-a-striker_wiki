## What the Build A Striker Build System Actually Is

**Build A Striker** is a fan-made Roblox soccer game by the group **Wild Card.**, themed as a tribute to Blue Lock. Its core hook is the build system: you don't pick a full squad or a full player — you construct one ultimate striker **one attribute at a time**, across ten rounds of decisions.

The verified loop is simple: roll a random team, receive a random player from that team, and then choose exactly **one** of that player's ten attributes to keep. That single choice is your entire "build" decision each round. Over ten rounds, those ten choices become your striker.

## The Ten Attributes You Can Draft

Each random player you receive has ten listed attributes, and you may take only one:

- **Shooting**
- **Speed**
- **Dribbling**
- **Passing**
- **Physical**
- **Defense**
- **Vision**
- **IQ**
- **Ego**
- **Weapon**

Important caveat: only these attribute *names* are officially documented. The game does not publish numeric values, effects, weights, or synergies for each attribute, so any guide claiming a specific "Shooting gives +X" breakdown is speculating. If you want a deeper look at the Blue Lock attributes the game draws from, the official [Blue Lock anime page](https://bluelock-pr.com/) context and fandom wiki cover the source material — but treat in-game stat effects as unverified until the developers document them.

## How a Round Plays Out, Step by Step

The build system runs on a repeated cycle across a match:

1. **Roll a random team** — teams range from First Selection all the way up to Pros, so the quality pool of your roll varies.
2. **Get a random player** from that team — you don't choose the player, only what you extract from them.
3. **Choose ONE attribute** from that player's ten options.
4. **Repeat for ten rounds**, then compare builds and climb the leaderboard.

Because both the team and the player are randomized, the system is heavily RNG-driven. Your skill comes from decision-making: given a random player, which single attribute best fills the gap in your current build?

## FAQ

**What is the Build A Striker build system?**
It's the game's core mechanic: across ten rounds, you roll a random team, receive a random player, and keep exactly one of that player's ten attributes — gradually constructing your ultimate striker.

**Which attribute should I pick first in Build A Striker?**
There's no verified tier list. All ten attributes (Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, and Weapon) are officially named, but their numeric effects aren't documented, so pick based on your build's gaps rather than claimed stat values.

**How many rounds are in a Build A Striker match?**
Ten rounds. After the tenth attribute choice, you climb the leaderboard based on your completed striker.

**Is Build A Striker an official Blue Lock game?**
No. The developers explicitly state it's a fan-made, unofficial tribute created by Blue Lock fans. It's published on Roblox by the group Wild Card.
