## What "Character Customization" Actually Means in Build A Striker

In [Build A Striker](https://www.roblox.com/games/98256320592611/), the Roblox soccer game by the Blue Lock fan studio Wild Card., customization isn't about outfits or sliders — it's about the striker you assemble stat by stat. Per the official game description, you "build the ultimate striker, one part at a time" by choosing a single attribute from each player you draft. That choice loop, repeated over 10 rounds, is the entire customization system.

The core loop, as verified from the official record, works like this:

1. **Roll a random team**, ranging from First Selection up to Pros.
2. **Receive a random player** from that team.
3. **Choose ONE of that player's 10 attributes** to add to your striker.
4. **Repeat across 10 rounds**, then climb the leaderboard.

## The 10 Attributes You Can Pick

Each drafted player offers exactly ten attribute options. These are the only customization choices the game officially documents:

- Shooting
- Speed
- Dribbling
- Passing
- Physical
- Defense
- Vision
- IQ
- Ego
- Weapon

What's *not* documented is just as important: the official page doesn't publish numeric stat values, how each attribute affects gameplay, tier lists, or attribute synergies. Any guide claiming exact formulas or "best" picks is speculating beyond what the developers have confirmed. Your safest approach is to experiment across runs and see which attributes fit how you like to play.

## How the Draft and Roll System Shapes Your Build

Because teams are rolled randomly — from First Selection all the way to the Pros tier — and players within that team are also random, you never fully control which attribute pool you're choosing from. That makes Build A Striker an RNG-driven draft game rather than a freeform character creator.

The strategy, then, is in your pick each round. A "Weapon" or "Ego" attribute from one player might tempt you, but if your striker already leans toward Shooting, another Shooting-adjacent pick could compound your strengths. Since the game doesn't document stacking effects, treat each round as a fresh decision informed by the build you've made so far.

## Rounds, Leaderboards, and Progression

A full run is **10 rounds**, after which you compare your completed striker on the leaderboard. The official description frames the goal as proving you're the "#1 Egoist" — a nod to the game's Blue Lock inspiration. The developers are explicit that this is a fan-made, unofficial tribute to the anime, so progression themes (Ego, First Selection) come straight from that source material.

As of the October 29, 2026 snapshot, the game had over 1.1 million visits, roughly 1,867 concurrent players, and a strongly positive vote ratio (7,939 up vs. 390 down) — a healthy sign the draft loop is landing with players.

## FAQ

**How does character customization work in Build A Striker?**
You customize your striker through drafting: roll a random team, get a random player, and choose one of that player's 10 attributes (Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, or Weapon) each round for 10 rounds.

**Can I pick any attribute I want every round?**
No. Your options are limited to the 10 attributes of the random player you receive after rolling a random team, so part of the game is adapting your build to what the RNG gives you.

**Is there a tier list for attributes in Build A Striker?**
Not officially. The developers haven't documented numeric values, effects, or rankings for the ten attributes, so any tier list you see is community speculation, not confirmed data.

**Is Build A Striker an official Blue Lock game?**
No. The developers describe it as a fan-made, unofficial tribute created by Blue Lock fans as a way to share their passion with the community.
