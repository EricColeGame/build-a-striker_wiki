## What Is Build A Striker?

Build A Striker is a fan-made Roblox soccer game by the group **Wild Card.**, created as a tribute to the anime Blue Lock. Its official tagline sums up the loop perfectly: build the ultimate striker, one part at a time. Instead of playing out full matches, you draft pieces of a superstar forward over ten rounds and compete to prove you're the "#1 Egoist."

The game launched in early October 2026 and has already passed one million visits, with thousands of likes and an active daily player base, so there are plenty of opponents to test your build against.

## How to Build a Striker: The Core Loop, Step by Step

The mechanics are simple, but every decision compounds over ten rounds. Here's the verified flow:

1. **Roll a random team.** Teams range from First Selection all the way up to the Pros, so the quality of your roll matters.
2. **Get a random player from that team.** You don't pick the player directly — the game does.
3. **Choose ONE of that player's 10 attributes.** This is the real strategy: which piece do you take for your striker?
4. **Repeat across 10 rounds.** Each round adds another part to your build.
5. **Climb the leaderboard** and prove your striker is the best in the lobby.

Because you can only take one attribute per round, the question each round isn't "who is this player?" — it's "which single trait is worth the most to my striker right now?"

## The 10 Attributes You Can Choose

Every player you draw has the same ten attribute slots. According to the official game record, they are:

- Shooting
- Speed
- Dribbling
- Passing
- Physical
- Defense
- Vision
- IQ
- Ego
- Weapon

Some names are self-explanatory (Shooting, Speed, Dribbling), while others like Ego and Weapon lean into the Blue Lock theme and are more mysterious. One thing worth knowing upfront: the game does **not** publicly document numeric stat values, attribute weights, or synergies. So rather than chasing hidden formulas, treat each pick as a straight comparison of which trait fills the biggest gap in your build.

## Strategy Tips for the Choice Loop

Since only the attribute names are verified, the safest winning approach is disciplined drafting rather than stat-chasing:

- **Build a balanced striker over ten rounds.** With only one pick per round, stacking four copies of similar traits leaves holes elsewhere.
- **Weigh risk versus floor.** Rolling a Pros-level team gives you a stronger pool, but a First Selection roll can still yield one attribute worth taking.
- **Don't ignore the unusual slots.** Ego and Weapon are part of every player's kit, and skipping them entirely means leaving picks on the table.
- **Track what you've taken.** Ten rounds go fast; knowing your remaining gaps makes each choice automatic.

Above all, remember the leaderboard rewards consistent builds across all ten rounds, not one lucky pick.
