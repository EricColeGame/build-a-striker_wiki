## What Are the Striker Stats in Build A Striker?

In [Build A Striker](https://www.roblox.com/games/98256320592611/), the striker stats you actually manage are the ten attributes shown on each player card: Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, and Weapon. These ten names are confirmed on the game's official Roblox page, and they're the foundation of the whole draft loop.

One important limit to set expectations: the game does not publicly document what the numbers on each attribute do — there are no official stat formulas, weights, or synergy values published by the developer, Wild Card. So treat any "best attribute" claim you see elsewhere as unconfirmed. What we can break down with confidence is how you acquire and choose these stats round by round.

## The Ten Attributes, Listed

Every player you draft brings a pool of ten possible attributes, and you pick exactly one per round:

- **Shooting** — finishing and goal-scoring ability
- **Speed** — pace on the pitch
- **Dribbling** — ball control in traffic
- **Passing** — distribution to teammates
- **Physical** — strength and stamina
- **Defense** — defensive contribution
- **Vision** — reading the field
- **IQ** — overall decision-making
- **Ego** — a nod to the Blue Lock inspiration, fitting for a game about proving you're the "#1 Egoist"
- **Weapon** — the game's signature special attribute

The exact gameplay effect of each stat isn't documented by the developer, so the names above reflect how the game presents them, not verified mechanics.

## How the Stat-Drafting Loop Works

Build A Striker follows a strict round structure — ten rounds, one stat choice each:

1. **Roll a random team.** Rolls span tiers from First Selection all the way up to Pros, mirroring the Blue Lock progression.
2. **Receive a random player from that team.** The RNG doesn't stop at the team level — the specific player is random too.
3. **Choose ONE of their ten attributes.** This is the core decision: which stat from this player do you add to your striker?
4. **Repeat for ten rounds**, then climb the leaderboard and see whose striker comes out on top.

Because both the team and the player are randomized, part of the skill is adapting: when the roll gives you a weak pool, you're deciding which of ten mediocre attributes hurts least, not just grabbing the obvious top pick.

## Game Overview: Creator, Popularity, and Blue Lock Roots

Build A Striker is a fan-made, unofficial tribute to the anime *Blue Lock*, created by the Roblox group [Wild Card](https://www.roblox.com/groups/17118136/Wild-Card) (owned by RemedyGod, with over 70,000 members at the time of writing). It supports up to 12 players per server and sits in the Sports genre.

The numbers show it's resonating: as of October 29, 2026, the game has logged over 1.1 million visits, roughly 1,850 concurrent players, more than 8,000 favorites, and a strongly positive vote ratio (about 7,939 upvotes to 390 downvotes). The 12-player lobby size means you're drafting your striker against real competition every match.

## Tips for Working With the Stat System

Since only the loop is verified, the best "strategy" advice is process advice:

- **Read all ten attributes before picking.** One attribute per player means your choice is irreversible within that round.
- **Judge pools, not single picks.** A First Selection roll with a deep attribute pool may beat a Pros roll with a thin one — though how team tiers affect stat quality isn't officially documented.
- **Track your build's shape across rounds.** With ten picks total, thinking a round or two ahead about what your striker is missing is the most reliable edge available.
