## What Build A Striker Is

Build A Striker is a fan-made Roblox soccer game inspired by Blue Lock, created by the group Wild Card. The premise is simple but addictive: instead of controlling a full match, you assemble the ultimate striker one attribute at a time. Every round, you roll a random team and a random player from that team, then you must choose exactly one of that player's attributes to add to your build.

The official [game page on Roblox](https://www.roblox.com/games/98256320592611/) describes the loop plainly — roll, receive a random player, pick one attribute, repeat across 10 rounds, and climb the leaderboard. At the time of writing, the game had surpassed 1.1 million visits with roughly 1,900 concurrent players, so it's clearly struck a chord with Blue Lock fans.

## The Core Striker Building Loop, Step by Step

Each run follows the same five-step structure:

1. **Roll a random team.** Teams range from First Selection all the way up to Pros, so the quality pool of your roll varies from round to round.
2. **Receive a random player** from that team. You don't get to pick the player — the roll decides who appears.
3. **Choose ONE attribute** from that player's kit. This is the only real decision you make each round, and it's what makes or breaks your striker.
4. **Repeat for 10 rounds.** Ten rolls, ten picks, ten chances to patch a weakness or double down on a strength.
5. **Climb the leaderboard** and prove you're the #1 Egoist.

Because the player pool is randomized, adaptability matters more than memorizing a fixed build order. A good striker building guide can only teach you the decision framework — the rolls themselves are up to RNG.

## The 10 Attributes You Can Draft

Every player who appears in your roll carries the same ten attribute categories, and you may only take one per pick:

- Shooting
- Speed
- Dribbling
- Passing
- Physical
- Defense
- Vision
- IQ
- Ego
- Weapon

One important note: the official game documentation verifies only the **names** of these ten attributes. Their numeric values, in-game effects, weights, and synergies are not publicly documented, so be wary of any guide claiming exact stat formulas. What you can plan around is the concept: draft broadly to cover your striker's gaps, or stack the same attribute when multiple high-tier players appear in your rolls.

## Team Tiers: First Selection to Pros

The roll pool spans the Blue Lock progression ladder, from the First Selection phase up through the Pros. Rolling a higher-tier team is your best shot at landing an elite attribute, but since the player is random within that team, a big team doesn't guarantee a big pick.

That tension — chase a higher tier or settle for a safe pick — is the strategic heart of each round. Ten rounds means you have room for a few gambles, but a streak of bad rolls late in a run can sink an otherwise strong striker.

## Tips for Smarter Picks

No official stat documentation exists, but the structure itself suggests a few sound habits:

- **Track what you've drafted.** With ten rounds and ten attribute categories, a balanced build is achievable if you pay attention to what's still missing.
- **Treat "Weapon" and "Ego" as premium picks.** These are signature Blue Lock concepts, and a random player carrying them is likely worth taking — though their exact effects aren't officially documented.
- **Don't autopick the same attribute every round.** Diversifying usually leaves fewer exploitable gaps by round 10.
- **Follow the community.** The official description points players to the community server for codes and updates. You can also join the [Wild Card Roblox group](https://www.roblox.com/groups/17118136/Wild-Card), which has over 70,000 members and is the creator group listed on the official game record.
