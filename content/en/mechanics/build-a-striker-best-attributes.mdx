If you're hunting for the "best attributes" in Build A Striker, here's the honest answer up front: the game doesn't publish numeric attribute effects, an official tier list, or any ranking of which picks are strongest. ## What Is Build A Striker?

Build A Striker is a fan-made Roblox soccer game created by the group Wild Card., built as a tribute to the Blue Lock anime. It launched on October 2, 2026, and according to the official Roblox game page, it has already crossed 1.1 million visits with a few thousand concurrent players at the time of writing. Up to 12 players compete in a match, and the game sits in the Sports & Racing genre.

The premise is simple: instead of controlling a full soccer match, you build the ultimate striker one piece at a time across ten rounds, then climb the leaderboard to prove you're the number one Egoist.

## The Complete List of Attributes

The official game description verifies ten named attributes. Each round, after you're dealt a random player, you choose ONE of that player's ten attributes to add to your striker:

- Shooting
- Speed
- Dribbling
- Passing
- Physical
- Defense
- Vision
- IQ
- Ego
- Weapon

That's the full roster of options — no hidden attributes are documented on the official page. Note that some of these, like Ego and Weapon, are clearly Blue Lock-flavored picks rather than traditional soccer stats, which fits the game's fan-made nature.

## How the Pick-One Loop Works

The official game record lays out the round structure step by step:

1. Roll a random team, ranging from First Selection all the way up to the Pros tier.
2. Get a random player from that team.
3. Choose one of that player's ten attributes.
4. Repeat across 10 rounds, then climb the leaderboard.

Because both the team and the player are randomized, your attribute choices are always made from whatever your roll gives you. Higher tiers like the Pros presumably offer stronger rolls, though the game doesn't document how tier quality affects attribute values.

## What the Game Doesn't Document (Yet)

In short, any claim that one attribute is objectively better than another isn't backed by the developers. Your best bet for up-to-date balance info is the game's official Roblox page and the Wild Card. community server mentioned in the description.

## Tips for Your Attribute Picks

Without official numbers, smart play comes from the structure of the loop itself:

- **Read your rolls.** Since teams and players are random, check what each dealt player offers before committing your pick — the ten attributes you see are your only options that round.
- **Think in rounds, not singles.** You get ten picks total, so each round's choice is one piece of a larger striker, not a standalone decision.
- **Watch the leaderboard.** Ten rounds determine your ranking, so consistent picks across all rounds matter more than any single pick.
- **Check for codes and updates.** The game was updated the same day this guide's data was collected, so the official page is worth rechecking — the developers direct players to their community server for codes and news.

## FAQ

### What are the 10 attributes in Build A Striker?
The officially listed attributes are Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, and Weapon. You choose one per round from the randomly dealt player.

### Which attribute is the best in Build A Striker?
There's no official answer. The developers have not published numeric attribute effects or a tier list, so any "best attribute" claim is unofficial opinion rather than documented fact.

### How many rounds are in a Build A Striker match?
Ten rounds. Each round you roll a team (from First Selection up to Pros), receive a random player, and pick one of that player's ten attributes before the leaderboard is decided.

### Is Build A Striker an official Blue Lock game?
No. Per its own disclaimer, it's a fan-made, unofficial tribute created by Blue Lock fans under the Roblox group Wild Card., which is run by owner RemedyGod.
