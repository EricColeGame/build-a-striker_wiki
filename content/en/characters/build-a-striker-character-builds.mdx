## What Build A Striker Character Builds Actually Are

Build A Striker is a fan-made Roblox soccer game inspired by Blue Lock, created by the group Wild Card. Instead of picking a preset class or character, your "build" is assembled round by round from randomly rolled players. Each round, you choose a single attribute from that player to add to your striker — so a character build here is simply the combination of ten attributes you've collected by the end of a match.

Because the game was released in early October 2026 and updates frequently, it's worth knowing exactly what's confirmed and what isn't. There is no official tier list, published roster, or documented numeric stat formula — so any guide claiming exact attribute values is speculation.

## The Core Gameplay Loop, Step by Step

The official game description lays out a clear five-step loop:

1. **Roll a random team** — draws range from First Selection all the way up to Pros, mirroring Blue Lock's progression.
2. **Get a random player** from that rolled team.
3. **Choose ONE of their 10 attributes** to add to your striker.
4. **Repeat across 10 rounds**, building your striker one part at a time.
5. **Climb the leaderboard** and prove you're the #1 Egoist.

Matches support up to 12 players, so you're competing against other egoists building strikers in the same lobby.

## The 10 Attributes You Can Pick

Every rolled player exposes the same ten named attributes. Your build is defined by which one you take from each player across the match:

- Shooting
- Speed
- Dribbling
- Passing
- Physical
- Defense
- Vision
- IQ
- Ego
- Weapon

The official documentation does not publish what each attribute does numerically, nor does it rank them. That's important: since rolls are random (RNG-based), part of the skill is adapting your picks to whatever players you're offered rather than forcing a fixed template.

## How to Think About Your Build Strategy

With no published stat values, the most reliable approach is flexible rather than locked-in:

- **Stay adaptable.** Since teams and players are random, rigid pre-planned builds will often be impossible. Decide your priorities but take the best available option each round.
- **Use all 10 rounds.** You get ten picks per match — every round is a chance to patch a gap in your build.
- **Learn the Blue Lock ladder.** Rolls span First Selection through Pros, so knowing the source material helps you recognize what caliber of player you've drawn.
- **Watch the lobby.** With up to 12 players per server, leaderboard competition means your picks matter relative to rivals, not in a vacuum.

## FAQ

There's no official tier list or ranking of attributes, so no build is officially "best." Builds are assembled from ten random rolls per match, so the strongest players adapt their picks each round rather than following a fixed template.

**How many attributes can I pick in Build A Striker?**
Each round you choose exactly one of ten attributes — Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, or Weapon — and a match lasts 10 rounds, giving you ten picks per build.
