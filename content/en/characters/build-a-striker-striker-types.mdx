## What Are Striker Types in Build A Striker?

If you're searching for striker types in **Build A Striker**, here's the honest answer up front: the game doesn't publish a fixed roster of striker classes, a tier list, or numeric attribute values. Instead, your striker's "type" is defined by which of the ten named attributes you pick across ten rounds. Every striker you build is essentially a custom combination of Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, and Weapon.

The game, a fan-made Blue Lock tribute by the Roblox group **Wild Card.**, launched in early October 2026 and has already crossed one million visits with a strongly positive vote ratio. Its core hook is simple: you don't choose your striker directly — RNG does.

## How the Striker-Building Loop Works

Each round follows the same verified cycle, straight from the official game description:

1. **Roll a random team** — rolls range from First Selection all the way up to Pros.
2. **Get a random player** from that team.
3. **Choose ONE of that player's 10 attributes** to add to your striker.
4. **Repeat across 10 rounds**, then climb the leaderboard and prove you're the #1 Egoist.

Because both the team and the player are random, no two builds come out the same. Your striker's "type" emerges from your choices — a pace-and-finishing build looks very different on paper than a Vision-and-IQ playmaker build, even if both came from the same random rolls.

## The 10 Attributes That Define Your Striker

These are the only verified attribute categories in the game. Each round, you may take exactly one:

- Shooting
- Speed
- Dribbling
- Passing
- Physical
- Defense
- Vision
- IQ
- Ego
- Weapon

One important note: the official page does **not** publish numeric effects for these attributes, and there's no official tier list ranking one attribute over another. Any guide claiming a "best attribute" is speculation, not official information. Treat attribute choice as a matter of the build identity you want, since ten rounds and ten attributes means a full build can touch every category once.

## Rounds, Leaderboard, and the Egoist Goal

A full match runs 10 rounds, matching the 10 available attributes. After the loop finishes, results feed into a leaderboard where you compete against other players in the same server (matches support up to 12 players). The "prove you're the #1 Egoist" framing is a direct nod to the Blue Lock anime that inspired the game — though it's worth remembering this is an unofficial fan project, not a licensed Blue Lock game.

## FAQ

**What attributes count as striker types in Build A Striker?**
The game defines striker builds through 10 attributes: Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, and Weapon. There are no officially named striker classes beyond these.

**How many rounds does it take to build a striker?**
Ten. Each round you roll a random team, receive a random player, and pick one of that player's ten attributes.

**Is there a best attribute in Build A Striker?**
No official tier list or attribute ranking has been published. The game presents all ten attributes as equal choices, one per round.

**Is Build A Striker an official Blue Lock game?**
No. It's a fan-made, unofficial tribute created by Blue Lock fans in the Wild Card. Roblox group.
