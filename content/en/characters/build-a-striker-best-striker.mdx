## What "Best Striker" Actually Means in Build A Striker

If you're searching for the best striker in Build A Striker, here's the honest answer up front: the game does not publish an official tier list, player ranking, or numeric attribute effects. What it does publish is a clear drafting loop — you roll a random team, get a random player from that team, and choose **one** of that player's ten attributes per round. Your "best striker" is therefore built from smart attribute choices across ten rounds, not from picking a fixed top-tier character.

The game is a fan-made Blue Lock tribute by the Roblox group Wild Card., and it's been growing fast since launching in early October 2026 — over 1.1 million visits and roughly 1,800 concurrent players as of October 29, 2026.

## The Verified Gameplay Loop, Step by Step

The official Roblox game page describes the core loop exactly like this:

1. **Roll a random team** — pools range from First Selection all the way up to Pros.
2. **Get a random player** from that team.
3. **Choose ONE of their 10 attributes** to take for your striker.
4. **Play 10 rounds**, then climb the leaderboard and prove you're the #1 Egoist.

Because both the team and the player are randomized, every run plays out differently. Your only real decision point each round is which single attribute to claim — that's where the strategy lives.

## All 10 Attributes You Can Draft

Each rolled player carries the same ten named attributes, and you can pick exactly one per round:

- Shooting
- Speed
- Dribbling
- Passing
- Physical
- Defense
- Vision
- IQ
- Ego
- Weapon

One important caveat: the official page lists these attribute names but does **not** document their numeric effects or how they interact. Any guide claiming exact stat values or ranking these attributes is guessing, so treat tier-list content with skepticism.

## Can You Actually Build a "Best" Striker?

Since no official rankings exist, the practical answer is: build around your picks, not around a meta. A few principles that follow directly from the verified rules:

- **You get 10 picks across 10 rounds**, so plan a balanced striker rather than stacking one style — though the game never states a required balance.
- **Higher-tier team pools** (First Selection through Pros) are rolled randomly, so luck plays a real role in which players you see.
- **Leaderboard performance is the goal**, and the game frames climbing it as proving you're the "#1 Egoist" — a nod to the Blue Lock theme.

If the developers later publish official attribute effects or rankings, that would change the picture — but as of today, nothing of the sort is documented.

## FAQ

### What is the best striker in Build A Striker?

There is no officially published best striker. The game randomizes teams and players and doesn't release a tier list or attribute rankings, so your strongest build depends on which attributes you draft across your 10 rounds.

### How many attributes can I pick in Build A Striker?

You choose one attribute per round out of ten options: Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, or Weapon. Over a full match you'll make 10 picks.
