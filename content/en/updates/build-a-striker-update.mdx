## What Build A Striker Is (Quick Refresher)

Build A Striker is a fan-made Roblox soccer game created by huge Blue Lock fans as a tribute to the anime. It's not an official Blue Lock product — the developer says so directly in the game's description. The core loop is a roll-and-build format:

- **Roll a random team**, ranging from First Selection all the way up to Pros
- **Get a random player** from that team
- **Choose ONE of that player's 10 attributes**: Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, or Weapon
- **Play 10 rounds** and climb the leaderboard to prove you're the #1 Egoist

Matches support up to 12 players, and the game sits in Roblox's Sports & Racing genre. The "[🧪 CHEMISTRY]" tag in the official title suggests chemistry is a featured system, though the page doesn't document how it works numerically.
