## The Core Gameplay Loop

Build A Striker launched on October 2, 2026 and has quickly grown past 1.1 million visits with roughly 1,800 players in-game at the time of this snapshot. The loop is simple, RNG-driven, and built for 10-round sessions:

- **Roll a random team** — the pool spans from First Selection all the way up to Pros
- **Receive a random player** from that rolled team
- **Choose ONE of the player's 10 attributes** to add to your striker: Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, or Weapon
- **Play 10 rounds** and climb the leaderboard to prove you're the #1 Egoist

Matches support up to 12 players, which gives the build-versus-compete format a competitive edge.
