## What Build A Striker Is

Build A Striker is a fan-made Roblox soccer game inspired by Blue Lock, created by the group Wild Card. The developer openly states on the game page that it's an unofficial tribute made by fans of the anime.

The core loop, as verified from the official description, works like this:

- **Roll a random team** — pulls range from First Selection all the way up to Pros tiers
- **Get a random player** from that rolled team
- **Choose ONE of the player's 10 attributes**: Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, or Weapon
- **Play 10 rounds** and climb the leaderboard to prove you're the #1 Egoist

The current title prefix, "[🧪 CHEMISTRY]," suggests chemistry is the featured mechanic or theme of this version of the game, though the developer hasn't published documentation explaining the system's specifics.

Since the game is RNG-driven, a few general approaches follow directly from the verified rules:
