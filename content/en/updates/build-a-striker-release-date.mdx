## What the Game Is About

The core loop, as described on the official page, works like this:

- **Roll a random team**, ranging from First Selection all the way up to Pros
- **Get a random player** from that rolled team
- **Choose one of that player's 10 attributes**: Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, or Weapon
- **Play 10 rounds** and climb the leaderboard to prove you're the #1 Egoist

In other words, you're building the ultimate striker one part at a time through RNG rolls and strategic attribute picks.
