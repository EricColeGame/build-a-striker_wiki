## Build A Striker Controls: The Short Answer

## Why This Game Needs Fewer Inputs Than You'd Expect

Build A Striker is a Blue Lock fan game focused on building the ultimate striker one part at a time, and its core loop is menu-driven rather than reflex-driven. Per the official description, each round works like this:

1. Roll a random team, from First Selection all the way up to Pros
2. Get a random player from that team
3. Choose ONE of their 10 attributes: Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, or Weapon
4. Play 10 rounds and climb the leaderboard

So most of your "controls" are taps and clicks in a draft-style interface, not movement combos. The official page doesn't document what the numeric attribute values actually do, so we can't tell you whether Speed beats Ego in a match — that information simply isn't published.
