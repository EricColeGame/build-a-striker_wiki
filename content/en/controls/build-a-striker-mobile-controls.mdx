## What Build A Striker Actually Is

Build A Striker is a fan-made Blue Lock tribute created by the group Wild Card. The loop is simple and menu-driven, which is why mobile play works well:

- Roll a random team, ranging from First Selection up to Pros
- Receive a random player from that team
- Choose **one** of that player's 10 attributes: Shooting, Speed, Dribbling, Passing, Physical, Defense, Vision, IQ, Ego, or Weapon
- Repeat across 10 rounds and climb the leaderboard

Because nearly every decision is a selection rather than a reflex input, the mobile experience mostly comes down to tapping the prompts and buttons the Roblox client renders on your screen.
